The Safe Creator Launchpad

Launching a YouTube channel should be exciting, not stressful. We give young creators the tools, graphics and confidence to stand out from day one, while setting up hard-hitting privacy shields to defend against cyberbullying, personal data exposure, and inappropriate content.

  • Physical Security Key & Account Hardening

    You get a physical hardware key to lock down your child’s account – preventing password theft and any unauthorized logins. Simply tap against your phone to unlock.

  • Parental Control & Comment Shields

    YouTube Studio setup directly on your device – featuring inbuilt auto-moderation while also giving you the ability to review comments before they ever go live.

  • Custom Channel Branding & Studio Art

    A complete visual makeover including a custom logo, channel banner and an animated video intro / outro – so your child’s channel looks legitimate and professional from day one.

  • Dedicated Storage Drive for Video Game Streamers

    High-speed USB storage drive pre-configured for budding video game streamers to easily save, organize and transfer their game clips to the editing studio – removing the risks of live streaming.

  • The Safe Upload Pipeline

    Third-party video uploading configured directly on your child’s device, bypassing the need for YouTube’s app. All uploads default to "Private" until you review them, giving your child full creative freedom while you retain 100% ownership and administrative control.

  • Privacy Shields & Separate Channels

    Device location metadata turned off on creator devices, combined with a dedicated public business email to keep all your family personal details private.

  • Device-Level Shielding & Filter App

    We install and configure a dedicated protection app on your child’s device. This directly protects their phone or tablet from dangerous content 24/7, without having to rely on your ISP.

  • Kids’ Safety & Anti-Phishing Parent Guides

    A physical internet safety guide for young creators to stick on their wall, paired with an anti-phishing technical cheat sheet for parents.

  • COPPA & Platform Compliance Setup

    We configure the channel’s privacy defaults to align with COPPA and YouTube safety guidelines – protecting your child’s privacy without you having to read through complex terms of service.
