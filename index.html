THE SAFE CREATOR LAUNCHPAD

We design custom security architecture for young creators and YouTubers - allowing them to express their creativity safely and giving parents complete peace of mind. We give children the tools, graphics and guidance to feel confident creating on YouTube, while setting up hard-hitting privacy shields to defend against cyberbullying, personal data exposure and inappropriate content.

  • The Safe Upload Pipeline

    Exclusive use of our custom-built YouTube uploader. This allows young creators to upload their videos without the need for any social media apps on their phone. All videos will upload as 'Private' until you review the content and decide to make it public.

  • The ComShell Key

    Access to the YouTube channel is locked on your phone behind a physical key - simply tap against the back of your phone to access all their content, review or delete comments and change their videos' visibility. This prevents any unauthorized logins and password theft - a common problem for young YouTubers.

  • Safe Browsing & Web Curfews

    Give your child safer internet access without locking down the whole house. We install custom network filtering directly onto your child's phone or tablet to automatically block explicit websites, malware and browser access to distracting platforms — with scheduled curfews you control.

  • Dedicated Storage Drive for Video Gamers

    Many young creators want to stream their video games, but linking Playstation or Xbox accounts directly to YouTube is risky. We provide a custom USB storage device that transfers videos between games console and mobile, allowing them to safely publish their recordings.

  • Professional Channel Branding

    Our in-house artists provide a complete visual makeover for the channel based on your child's interests. We design logos, banners, watermarks and custom branding to ensure their channel looks legitimate and professional from day one.

  • Proactive YouTube Moderation

    Alongside our custom Safe Upload Pipeline, we set up strict multi-layered channel moderation. We configure automated comment filtering, block inappropriate keywords and disable risky interactive elements to massively reduce the chances of cyberbullying and negative content from ever reaching your child.

  • Mini Wall Poster & Parent Guide

    We provide young creators with a simple internet-safety poster for their wall, to ensure they know the basics of digital hygiene before they begin creating content. We also provide parents with an anti-phishing cheat sheet - if the channel becomes successful, our guide ensures you can protect yourself against threats and scams.

Why ComShell?

Creative self-expression is vital for young people, but fear of online safety risks shouldn’t hold them back. Founded by a cybersecurity specialist with a background in journalism, podcasting and brand design - ComShell bridges the gap between digital defence and media production.
